Malin’s Quest: Interactive Narrative Adventure

Jelajahi kisah interaktif legenda Malin Kundang dalam bahasa Inggris. Setiap keputusan moral, pemahaman kosakata, serta ketepatan struktur teks dan tata bahasa menentukan akhir petualangan serta perolehan lencanamu!

Capaian & Tujuan Pembelajaran (Fase F – Kelas XII)

  • Fungsi Sosial & Struktur Teks: Mengidentifikasi bagian Orientation, Complication, Climax, dan Resolution pada Narrative Text.
  • Unsur Kebahasaan: Memahami penggunaan Simple Past Tense dan kosakata kontekstual melalui fitur Smart Glossary.
  • Berpikir Kritis (HOTS): Mengevaluasi nilai moral cerita serta mencapai ketuntasan belajar (KKTP ≥ 75).
💡 Tips Bermain: Klik kata berwarna kuning (seperti ini) di dalam cerita untuk membuka arti kontekstual pada Smart Glossary.
Babak 1: Orientation – Desa Pesisir Air Manis
Babak 1 dari 4 • Orientation & Vocabulary

The Humble Beginning

📌 Instruksi: Baca paragraf di bawah ini, klik kata yang disorot untuk melihat artinya, lalu jawab pertanyaan pemahaman struktur teks.

Long ago, in a quiet coastal village in West Sumatra, there lived a poor widow named Mande Rubayah and her only son, Malin Kundang. After his father passed away at sea, Malin grew up as a diligent and courageous young man.

Every morning, Malin helped his mother gather firewood and catch fish near the harbor. Although their life was simple, Mande Rubayah always prayed for her son's bright future and taught him to stay humble wherever he went.

Tantangan Babak 1 (20 Poin): Berdasarkan struktur Narrative Text, apa fungsi utama bagian cerita di atas?
Babak 2: Complication – Pelayaran Kapal Saudagar
Babak 2 dari 4 • Complication, Moral Choice & Grammar

The Voyage of Ambition

📌 Instruksi: Tentukan pilihan keputusan moral Malin di perantauan, lalu lengkapi kalimat Simple Past Tense di bawahnya.

One day, a large merchant ship anchored near the village. Malin saw a golden opportunity to change his family's destiny. With a heavy heart, Mande Rubayah allowed him to sail away.

Years passed. Through hard work and intelligence, Malin became a wealthy captain and married a noblewoman. When his grand ship finally returned to his hometown, villagers recognized him and quickly informed his aging mother.

Keputusan Moral Bercabang (10 Poin): Saat Mande Rubayah berlari memanggil namanya di dermaga, sikap apa yang seharusnya diambil Malin?
Tantangan Grammar – Simple Past Tense (20 Poin): Lengkapi kalimat berikut dengan bentuk kata kerja lampau yang tepat!

"When Mande Rubayah _______ Malin's scar on his arm, she immediately _______ that he was her beloved son."

Critical Thinking & Moral Reflection

📌 Instruksi: Jawab soal analisis tingkat tinggi (HOTS) berikut untuk menentukan skor akhir dan lencana pencapaianmu!

In the traditional ending, Malin's arrogance led to his petrified fate on the shore. However, in modern storytelling, readers are invited to reflect on why pride often blinds people from appreciating those who sacrificed for their success.
